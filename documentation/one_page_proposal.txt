FOOD DELIVERY PERFORMANCE & CUSTOMER ANALYTICS PIPELINE
Phase 1 – One-Page Project Proposal

Dataset:
A synthetic relational food-delivery dataset containing customers, restaurants, menu items, orders, order items, delivery details and customer reviews. The dataset contains more than 50,000 records and is designed with realistic data-quality issues such as duplicate orders, orphan records, invalid numeric values and missing ratings.

Pipeline:
Raw CSV files will be ingested into Databricks Bronze without changing the source data. Silver will perform type conversion, duplicate removal, referential-integrity checks, missing-value handling and standardisation. Gold will contain business-ready aggregates. Gold data will then be exported and loaded into Snowflake through a stage and COPY INTO for SQL analysis.

Three Business Questions:
1. Which restaurants have the highest late-delivery rates, and how does late delivery relate to customer ratings?
2. Which customer segments contribute the most revenue, and how does their ordering behaviour change over time?
3. Which food categories generate the highest revenue, and which categories receive the strongest customer ratings?

Silver Layer:
Silver will contain cleaned and typed customer, restaurant, menu-item, order, order-item, delivery and review data. Duplicate orders will be removed using order_id, orphan order-item records will be rejected, invalid numeric values will be converted safely and missing review ratings will be retained as NULL where appropriate. Cleaning decisions will be documented.

Expected Outcome:
The completed pipeline will provide trusted food-delivery performance and customer analytics through Databricks and Snowflake, with reproducible data-quality checks and SQL answers to the three business questions.
